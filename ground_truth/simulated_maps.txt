the simulation map generation file (sim_lang_map.py)

-----------------------------------------------

## Ground truth 
The bash script was developed to generate simulated language-related Z-maps with predefined patterns of hemispheric lateralization. 
For each run of bash script, 10 maps were randomly generated. 
Predefined lateralization classes: 
  strong leftward lateralization
  moderate leftward lateralization
  weak leftward lateralization
  bilateral organization
  weak rightward lateralization
  moderate rightward lateralization
  strong rightward lateralization.
